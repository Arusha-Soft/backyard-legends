ART STYLE:

For the “street-style” vibe, it’s more about the energy and culture of real-life Spades games rather than a literal street environment.

Think:
- Dark mode UI (charcoal/black base)
- Bold, high-contrast colors (gold, red, green accents)
- Clean but strong design — not cartoonish
- Subtle textures (table/concrete feel)
- Emphasis on competitive, high-pressure gameplay

For multiplayer:
- The plan is real-time multiplayer (not turn-based delay)
- 4 players total (2v2 teams)
- Cross-platform (iOS + Android in the same matchmaking pool)

That said, I’m approaching this in phases:

Phase 1 (current focus):
- Core Spades game logic (4-player, 2v2)
- Custom rules:
  - Minimum team bid = 4  
  - No Nil unless losing by more than 150 points  
  - Only the losing team can bid Nil (regular Nil only)  
- Game modes: first to 100, 200 (ranked), and 500  
- Full turn system, trick resolution, and scoring  
- Basic UI

Phase 2:
- Real-time multiplayer
- Matchmaking system
- Ranking system
